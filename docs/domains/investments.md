# Investments Domain

Status: APPROVED

Domain: Investments

Version: V4

---

# Purpose

Investments represent assets that may gain or lose value over time.

Investments are different from Savings.

Investments are not intended for money storage.

Investments are intended for wealth growth and capital appreciation.

---

# Core Rule

Investments

≠

Savings

Savings preserve money.

Investments expose money to market risk.

---

# Examples

Investment Assets:

* Gold
* Stocks
* Mutual Funds
* ETFs
* Bonds
* Crypto Assets
* Real Estate Investments

---

# Non-Investments

Examples:

* Emergency Fund
* Vacation Savings
* Home Safe
* Cash Box
* Savings Bank Account

These are Saving Accounts.

Not Investments.

---

# Gold Rule

Gold is an Investment Asset.

Gold must never be represented as a Saving Account.

Reason:

Gold value changes over time.

Gold belongs to the Investment Domain.

---

# Investment Ownership

Investments belong to the Accounts Layer.

Investment Accounts own money.

Investment Accounts affect:

* Net Worth
* Asset Allocation
* Financial Position

---

# Investment Accounts

Examples:

* Gold Portfolio
* Stock Portfolio
* Crypto Portfolio
* Brokerage Account

These are real Accounts.

---

# Investment Transactions

Examples:

* Buy Asset
* Sell Asset
* Deposit Funds
* Withdraw Funds

Investment activity must be represented using Transactions.

---

Investment Accounts:

# Net Worth

Investments affect Net Worth.

Current valuation contributes to total assets.

---

# Valuation Rule

Investment value may change over time.

Examples:

Gold

Purchase Value = 10000

Current Value = 12000

---

Stock

Purchase Value = 5000

Current Value = 4500

---

Forecasting and Analytics may use both values.

---

# Relationship With Forecasting

Forecasting may include:

* Asset Growth
* Asset Decline
* Portfolio Value

Forecasting reads Investments directly.

---

# Relationship With AI

AI may:

* Explain performance
* Analyze allocation
* Suggest diversification

AI must never execute investment transactions automatically.
