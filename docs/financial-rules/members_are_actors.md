# Members Are Actors

Status: APPROVED

Category: Shared Finance Rule

Version: V4

---

# Purpose

Define the role of Members inside Shared Finance Architecture.

This rule protects Wafferly from introducing Member Balances or Member-Owned Money.

---

# Rule

Members are actors.

Members are not money containers.

Members do not own balances.

Members do not own money.

Accounts always own money.

---

# Member Responsibilities

Members may act as:

* Owner
* Participant
* Contributor
* Permission Holder

A Member represents a person interacting with financial entities.

A Member does not represent a financial container.

---

# Examples

Correct:

Family Account
Balance = 20000

Members:

* Ahmed
* Sara
* Ali

Result:

Family Account owns 20000

Members own nothing.

---

Incorrect:

Ahmed Balance = 5000

Sara Balance = 3000

Ali Balance = 12000

Reason:

Money ownership belongs to Accounts.

Not Members.

---

# Shared Accounts

Shared Accounts are real Accounts.

Examples:

* Family Account
* Couple Account
* Business Account

Accounts own money.

Members interact with Accounts.

---

# Shared Goals

Shared Goals support contribution tracking.

Example:

Vacation Goal

Ahmed = 5000

Sara = 3000

Ali = 2000

These values represent contribution tracking.

They do not represent Member-owned balances.

---

# Shared Budgets

Shared Budgets support:

* Contribution Tracking
* Usage Tracking
* Overspending Analysis

Per Member.

Budget ownership still belongs to the Budget.

Money ownership still belongs to Accounts.

---

# Shared Liabilities

Shared Liabilities support ownership percentages.

Example:

Home Loan

Ahmed = 50%

Sara = 50%

or

Car Loan

Ahmed = 70%

Sara = 30%

This represents responsibility allocation.

Not money ownership.

---

# Permissions

Supported Roles:

* Owner
* Editor
* Viewer

Permissions control access.

Permissions do not affect ownership.

---

# Net Worth Rule

Supported Views:

* Individual Net Worth
* Family Net Worth

These are analytical views.

They do not create Member-owned money.

---

# Relationship With Accounts

Accounts own money.

Members interact with money.

Accounts remain the financial owner.

---

# Relationship With Shared Finance

Shared Finance introduces:

* Participation
* Ownership Tracking
* Contribution Tracking
* Permission Management

Shared Finance does not introduce:

* Member Accounts
* Member Wallets
* Member Balances

---

# Summary

Members are actors.

Accounts own money.

Members may:

* Participate
* Contribute
* Manage
* View

Members never own money.
