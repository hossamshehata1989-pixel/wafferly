# M2.4 — Ledger Mapping and Projection Completeness

**Status:** Implemented and runtime-verified in the complete Flutter project.
**Verification date:** 2026-11-01.

## Architectural boundary

- ADR-016 remains **Accepted**.
- `FinancialTransactionRecord` remains the Financial Write Model.
- `JournalEntryMutation` remains Accounting Intent; it is not persisted as the Ledger.
- `LedgerEntry` remains a persisted projection produced by `LedgerProjectionService` through `TransactionLedgerBuilder` and `LedgerPort`.
- ADR-017 remains **Proposed** and is not implemented by this change.

## Mapping contract

1. Every configured expense and income category has an explicit Chart of Accounts mapping in `FinancialEngineBootstrap`.
2. `ChartOfAccounts` does not infer an `expense_default` mapping. Missing accounting-intent mappings fail with `MissingChartOfAccountsMappingException`.
3. `LedgerAccountSeeder` ensures one correctly typed system Ledger account for each configured category and creates stable system equity accounts for opening balances and balance reconciliation.
4. `CategoryLedgerMapper` validates that the persisted category mapping points to a system Ledger account of the expected type.
5. A missing or incorrectly typed Ledger mapping fails before projection persistence. When reached through a Financial Engine mutation, the Unit of Work runs registered compensating rollback callbacks.

## Projection contract

| Financial write model | Ledger projection |
|---|---|
| Expense | 2 balanced entries: expense debit, source-account credit |
| Income | 2 balanced entries: destination-account debit, income credit |
| Transfer | 2 balanced entries: destination debit, source credit |
| Credit Card charge | 2 balanced entries: expense debit, card-liability credit |
| Opening balance — asset | 2 balanced entries: asset debit, `opening_balance_equity` credit |
| Opening balance — liability | 2 balanced entries: `opening_balance_equity` debit, liability credit |
| Balance reconciliation | 2 balanced entries using `balance_reconciliation_equity` |
| Correction | 4 balanced entries: reversal of the before-state and projection of the after-state |
| Invalidation | 2 balanced reversal entries |

Projection batches must contain 2 or 4 entries, matching debit and credit line counts, finite non-negative amounts, and equal debit/credit totals before persistence. An existing projection is treated as an idempotent replay only when its expected entry count and balance checks pass; partial groups fail rather than being silently accepted.

## Credit Card expense policy

A Credit Card purchase is an expense on the purchase date. Its Ledger debit uses `LedgerPurpose.expense`; the credit to the Credit Card liability uses `LedgerPurpose.debt`. A later card payment remains a liability settlement/transfer, not a second expense.

## Integrity diagnostics

- Projection groups are checked by projection ID, not assumed to equal one Transaction ID.
- Ordinary transaction and invalidation groups require 2 balanced entries.
- Correction groups require 4 balanced entries.
- Correction and invalidation projection IDs are resolved through their persisted Hive boxes for orphan detection.
- Persisted correction or invalidation records with no corresponding Ledger projection group are reported as integrity issues.
- Projection lines with blank account IDs are reported as integrity issues.
- Corrected transaction IDs are not reported as missing their own Ledger group because the replacement truth is projected under the correction ID.
- Opening balances and balance reconciliations are included in the supported transaction types checked for missing projections.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Drift implementation or schema migration.
- ADR-017 adoption or persistent Journal storage.
- `PurchaseGroup` and installment workflow redesign.
- Workflow ID, `operationId`, or temporary idempotency-key redesign.
- Durable crash-atomic Unit of Work. The current `MemoryFinancialUnitOfWork` performs compensating rollback; it is not a database transaction.
